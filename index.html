Part 1: Review Security Controls

Security controls can be divided into three types:

  1. 1.

    Physical security controls

    - implemented to control physical access to people, equipment, facilities, and information.

  2. 2.

    Technical security controls

    - implemented to protect hardware and software systems and the information that these systems transmit, process, or store.

  3. 3.

    Administrative security controls

    - are policies, procedures, rules, and guidelines that are followed by personnel in order to achieve the security goals of an organization.

Security controls are viewed as having three functions:

  1. 1.

    Preventive

    - stop security threats from occurring.

  2. 2.

    Detective

    - identify unauthorized activity.

  3. 3.

    Corrective

    - address unwanted activity by restoring systems to normal CIA status.

Reflection Questions

1. Why are preventive physical controls important in schools?

  • Preventive physical controls are essential because they help to keep students and staff safe from physical hazards. They also keep unauthorized individuals out of restricted areas and safeguard computers, servers, and other equipment from damage or theft. For example, schools may use door locks, CCTV cameras, and access cards to increase security and ensure everyone’s safety.

2. What preventive administrative controls are most effective against social engineering, including vectors that spread ransomware?

  • One of the most successful administrative controls to prevent social engineering and ransomware attacks is security awareness training. It teaches staff and students how to recognize suspicious emails, avoid clicking unknown links, and avoid downloading unsafe attachments. Schools must also have security policies in place and educate staff on the importance of keeping their information and passwords secure. All these practices can lower the possibility of attackers getting into school systems.

3. What is essential to preventing lasting damage from ransomware attacks while saving money on ransomware payments for restoration of data?

  • Regular and reliable data backups are crucial to avoiding permanent damage from ransomware attacks. Backups enable schools to recover important files without having to pay the ransomware attackers to unlock them. In addition, schools should also promote the practice of staff saving work on secure network servers rather than on individual computers. Backups should be tested periodically and stored offsite to allow them to be minimally impacted by ransomware.
